B · Crisp

High-contrast and technical, in the spirit of Vercel. True black, white as the accent, color reserved for severity only. Numbers in a monospace face; everything sits in sharp, bordered grids.

Today's identity, disciplined. Keeps the indigo and the cards, drops the glow, and puts every card, tile and badge on one shared size and spacing scale. Softer and friendlier than A or B.
